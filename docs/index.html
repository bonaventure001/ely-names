Your name on Elysium

Register a .ely name and link it to your wallet. Use it to send and receive crypto on Elysium instead of a long 0x address.

FAQ
What is a .ely name?

A short, readable name linked to your wallet address. People can send crypto to yourname.ely instead of copying a long 0x address.

How much does a name cost?

The price is per year, depends on the length of the name (shorter names cost more), and is shown in HYPE before you confirm anything. Renewals are charged at the full price.

How long does a name last?

One year. You can renew from the Your names list at any time, and anyone can renew a name. After a name expires you have 90 days to renew it. After that, anyone can register it.

Why are there two wallet confirmations?

Registering takes two steps. The first reserves your choice, and the second completes the registration about 18 seconds later. This stops others from taking a name they spotted in a pending transaction.

Is a name an NFT?

Yes. Every name is an NFT you own. You can send it to another wallet or sell it, and ownership moves to the new holder along with a fresh set of records. Expired names cannot be transferred. A 3% royalty applies to resales on marketplaces that support royalties.

What is a primary name?

The name shown for your wallet. After registering, open Your names and choose Make primary.

Who gets a free or discounted name?

Partner projects can offer their communities a free or discounted first year. Holders of the Kaola NFT at the announced snapshot can claim one free name per Kaola, and other partners, such as an NFT project or a DeFi platform, can reward their holders or most active users. Eligibility comes from a public list published with each offer, and each entry can be used once. If your wallet is on a list, the offer is applied automatically when you connect it.

Why can’t I register a certain name?

It may already be taken, or it may be a protected word, such as a brand name. If a protected name belongs to your project, contact support.

Is this live on mainnet?

Not yet. The service currently runs on the Elysium testnet. Names registered here are for testing and will not carry over to mainnet.

Which wallets work?

Browser wallets like MetaMask, and the built-in browser inside wallet apps. On a phone, open this page from inside your wallet app.

Something went wrong. What do I do?

Check that your wallet is on Elysium Testnet and has enough HYPE for gas, then try again. If it still fails, contact support on X.

Wallet safety

Your names belong to your wallet, so there is no separate account to change. If you are moving to a new wallet, send your names to it below. If your wallet is already compromised, an attacker may be able to move your names too, so act quickly and only from a wallet you still control.
